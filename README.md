# HumanByDesignLab

### Exploring AI for work, with people at the centre.

Hi, I'm **Lieven** — an **HRBP at CERN**, a **PhD researcher**, and a **master's graduate in Artificial Intelligence**.

My work brings together HR practice, research and applied AI, with a particular interest in **computational linguistics, natural language processing and machine learning**. I'm interested in how we understand people and performance at work, how language shapes that understanding, and how technology can support better HR practice.

**HumanByDesignLab** is my personal space to connect these interests through research workflows, practical experiments and shared learning.

## Research and current explorations

I'm currently doing work with **computational linguistics, natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning applied to HR data (qualitative and quantitative)**.

This brings together my experience in HR with my technical interests: analysing workplace language, exploring patterns in text, and interpreting computational results in their organisational context.

**My PhD direction is still evolving.** Employability and career development remain part of my broader research interests, alongside performance management, language and AI in organisations. I'm using this space to explore these connections as my research develops.

## From HR practice to applied AI

My HR work at CERN spans recruitment and selection, leadership performance and talent management, and support to managers and staff. It gives me a practical perspective on the questions I study and the tools I want to explore.

Alongside my research, I'm interested in:

- **AI in everyday HR:** useful applications for professionals, managers and employees.
- **Human–AI collaboration:** supporting judgement, evaluating outputs and keeping people involved in consequential decisions.
- **AI and careers:** how automation, algorithmic hiring and AI literacy may influence employability.
- **Workflow automation:** reducing repetitive administration and improving follow-up.

## Research tools and workflows

My current technical work centres on **NLP and machine learning for analysing performance management text**. I aim to connect computational analysis with careful interpretation and an understanding of the HR context.

My research workflows also include Python-based literature exploration and bibliometric analysis using Scopus data, with an interest in NLP, clustering and topic modelling to help navigate research literature.

## Connecting and collaborating

I'm using this lab to develop and share research tools, experiments and reusable workflows as they take shape.

I'd be glad to connect with people working on **computational linguistics, NLP, machine learning, performance management, employability and human-centred AI**.
